Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our AI Video Creation service.

What is included in the ₹1,500 AI video creation package?

The ₹1,500 package covers creative concept drafting, prompt design, high-definition AI visual generation, synchronized voiceover narration, background music/sound effects, revision rounds, and complete commercial usage rights.

How long does delivery take for an AI video?

Most single-scene or short social video briefs are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Multi-scene storyboards, custom character series, or longer productions are typically delivered in 2 to 4 business days.

Can you provide vertical videos for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. We specifically tailor videos for 9:16 vertical (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok) and 16:9 widescreen (YouTube, desktop websites, presentations). Let us know your target channel during briefing.

Do I have full commercial rights to use the AI video?

Yes, absolutely. Once delivered, you receive full commercial rights with zero recurring royalties. You can use your video on social channels, paid marketing ads, websites, or client presentations.
